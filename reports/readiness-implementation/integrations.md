## Growth email interface (requested follow-up)

`server/services/emailService.ts` exports:

* `sendOnce(request: SendOnceRequest): Promise<SendOnceResult>` and `emailService.sendOnce(request)`.
* Request: stable `operationKey`, `to`, `from`, `subject`, and `html` and/or `text`.
* Result discriminant: `status: "accepted"` with `messageId` and `operationKey`; `"rejected"` with `error`; or `"unknown"` with `error`.

The implementation delegates to pure `emailSendOnce.ts`, passes Resend's supported `idempotencyKey` option, invokes the provider **once**, never queues/retries, and does not convert ambiguous transport errors to retryable `false`. Accepted means provider queued, not delivered. **Resend's documented idempotency retention is 24 hours**: growth must persist operation payload/key/receipt and quarantine unknown outcomes rather than retrying indefinitely or generating a new key. Growth owns wiring campaign callers; no sibling files were edited. Existing legacy boolean `EmailService` methods were not silently changed to this new contract.
