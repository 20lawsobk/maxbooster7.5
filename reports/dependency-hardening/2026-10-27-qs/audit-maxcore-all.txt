It is a regression introduced by PR #1004 in 14.0.0: 13.0.2 processed a 313 KB input in 125 ms, while every version since takes about 11 seconds on the same input. At the sizes below, 200 KB takes 3.6 seconds, 400 KB takes 18.1 seconds, and 800 KB takes 74.0 seconds, which is roughly 4–5× per doubling. No options are required to trigger it.\n\n### Details\n\nsrc/rules_block/reference.ts appends continuation lines with str += lineContent at lines 74, 83, 100, 133, and 150, then keeps indexing the accumulated string via str.charCodeAt(pos) in the label and whitespace loops and str.slice(...) in parseLinkTitle.
