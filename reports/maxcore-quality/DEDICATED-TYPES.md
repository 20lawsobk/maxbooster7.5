# Dedicated type outputs — original 2026-09-23-all-types trials

This assessment covers **recorded outputs only** in [`2026-09-23-all-types`](2026-09-23-all-types) and the separately labeled eight-case [`2026-09-23-types-corrected`](2026-09-23-types-corrected) follow-up, against the requests and criteria in [`types.json`](../../evaluations/maxcore-quality/types.json). These were in-process handler trials, **not** HTTP/auth, publishing, scheduling, or end-to-end product tests. “Returned” means the handler returned something, not that the requested work was completed or that the checkpoint generated it. No new model runs were made for this report. Original and corrected result JSON and media remain unchanged.

## Evidence and judgment rules

- Text must be readable, relevant, in the requested form, and free of invented claims; campaign must be a coherent, usable two-week Instagram rollout. The supplied *fictional* release has no known chart position, stream totals, awards, six-month secret, or actual trending success. A response `source: "awareness"`, an awareness-composed `agent_outputs` item, and “model_ready” are not proof of successful checkpoint-authored text. `confidence` and `quality_score` in responses are self-reported, not this assessment.
- Image success requires an actual decodable image in the requested aspect ratio **and** recognizable lanterns/harbor and mellow mood; pathtraced success additionally requires observed tracer execution. Audio success requires decodable ~4-second requested-format, non-silent, unclipped audio **and** musical match to the brief. A file or metadata cannot establish audible musical fidelity or checkpoint inference. Video success requires a completed decodable multi-frame video of short requested duration with the requested subject, not just a job, script, or spec. Spec success means a nonempty suitable concept/script; `asset://` is an identifier for a proposed asset, **not rendered media**.
- The first five original cases (`text-api`, `text-slots`, `content-generic`, `content-api`, `social-variants`) preceded the all-threads profiler. Their empty `agent_outputs`/`awareness`/`exceptions` arrays **do not establish the absence** of work in worker threads. Judge their outputs, declared source and return/error status; do not infer their underlying inference path from those missing observations. Later recorded traces do expose repeated `ValueError: too many values to unpack (expected 3)` at `forward_with_kv`/`prefill`, often followed by `source: "awareness"` text. That is a **real recurrent model-attention failure**, distinct from an evaluator protection failure. Backend attachment, weights-loaded messages, operation counts and `source: "prior"` technique do not prove successful creative inference.
