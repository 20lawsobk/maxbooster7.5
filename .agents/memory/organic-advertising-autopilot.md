---
name: Organic advertising autopilot
description: Max Booster's advertising autopilot is MaxCore-powered organic promotion through each user's connected social profiles, not paid ad placement.
---

Max Booster's advertising system and advertising autopilot use MaxCore to create organic promotion for each account's connected social profiles. They do not buy paid ad inventory or spend an ad budget. The social profiles are the distribution conduits.

**Why:** The product owner clarified this design on 2026-11-17.

**How to apply:** Keep campaign generation and distribution account-scoped, use the user's connected social channels, and do not introduce paid-ad spend or treat budget fields as permission to spend.
