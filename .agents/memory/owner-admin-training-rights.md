---
name: Owner training and generation rights
description: Scope and handling of the owner's standing authorization for Max Booster training and generation.
---

The user grants standing authorization for training and generation using their data and sources they own or control; do not ask them to repeat it. This includes owner-admin-authored content and MaxCore's own simulation data. It does not grant rights to third-party material the owner does not control. Continue to require privacy, quality, independent evaluation, candidate certification, and serving-promotion gates.

Content that merely includes public or third-party awareness context is not automatically authorized for weight training. Exclude mixed-source text unless its third-party material has separate documented training permission; public visibility alone is not permission. Keep source provenance and rights evidence attached through candidate creation.

**Why:** the user explicitly granted standing training and generation rights for their own controlled content; third-party social content still requires its own permission.

**How to apply:** treat owner-authored and owner-controlled content as authorized for training and generation when its provenance is verifiable. Do not use the grant to bypass candidate gates or broaden rights to ordinary users or third-party source text.
