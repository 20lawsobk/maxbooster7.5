---
name: Owner-generated training rights
description: Scope and handling of the user's training authorization for Max Booster admin-generated content.
---

The user granted standing authorization for MaxCore training across the owner's data and training sources they own or control; do not ask them to repeat it. This includes owner-admin-authored content and MaxCore's own simulation data. It does not grant rights to third-party material the owner does not control. Continue to require privacy, quality, independent evaluation, candidate certification, and serving-promotion gates.

Content that merely includes public or third-party awareness context is not automatically authorized for weight training. Exclude mixed-source text unless its third-party material has separate documented training permission; public visibility alone is not permission. Keep source provenance and rights evidence attached through candidate creation.

**Why:** the user explicitly granted training rights for their own admin-generated content, while prior requirements prohibit assuming third-party social content is trainable.

**How to apply:** treat owner-authored generation as an eligible rights class only when the authenticated owner-admin origin is verifiable. Do not use the grant to bypass candidate gates or broaden rights to ordinary users or third-party source text.
